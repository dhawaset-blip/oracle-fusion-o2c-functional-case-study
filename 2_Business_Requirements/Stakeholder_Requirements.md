# Stakeholder Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the Order-to-Cash process and summarizes their business needs.

## 2. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in O2C | Primary Need |

| Customer | Places order and makes payment | Accurate order processing and timely fulfillment |
| Sales Representative | Manages customer order | Ability to capture and track customer orders |
| Order Management Team | Validates and processes orders | Accurate and controlled order processing |
| Warehouse Team | Prepares products | Clear fulfillment requirements and product availability |
| Shipping Team | Delivers products | Accurate shipping information |
| Accounts Receivable Team | Manages customer invoices and payments | Accurate invoicing and payment tracking |
| Finance Team | Manages financial processing | Reliable transaction and payment information |
| Business Analyst | Analyzes and documents business needs | Clear requirements and process validation |
| Business Management | Monitors business performance | Visibility into order, revenue and payment status |

## 3. Stakeholder Expectations

### Customer

The customer expects:

- Correct order processing
- Accurate pricing
- Timely fulfillment
- Correct shipment
- Accurate invoice
- Easy payment processing

### Sales Team

The sales team needs:

- Customer information
- Product information
- Order entry capability
- Order status visibility
- Ability to identify order issues

### Order Management

The Order Management team needs:

- Valid customer information
- Valid product information
- Correct quantities and prices
- Order validation controls
- Order status tracking

### Warehouse

The warehouse team needs:

- Confirmed order information
- Product and quantity details
- Fulfillment instructions
- Shipment information

### Accounts Receivable

The Accounts Receivable team needs:

- Accurate customer invoice information
- Order and fulfillment information
- Payment status
- Outstanding balance information

### Finance

Finance needs:

- Reliable transaction information
- Accurate payment records
- Reconciliation information
- Visibility into outstanding receivables

## 4. Key Business Need

All stakeholders require a standardized process in which customer-order information can move consistently from order creation through fulfillment, invoicing and payment processing.
