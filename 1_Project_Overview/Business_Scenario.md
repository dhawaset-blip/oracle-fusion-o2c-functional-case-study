# Primary Business Scenario

## Scenario ID

**O2C-SCN-001**

## Customer

**ABC Solutions Pvt. Ltd.**

## Supplier

**NovaTech Electronics Pvt. Ltd.**

## Business Situation

ABC Solutions needs to purchase computer equipment for its employees and places an order with NovaTech Electronics.

## Customer Order

| Product | Quantity | Unit Price | Line Value |

| Business Laptop | 10 | ₹60,000 | ₹6,00,000 |
| 24-inch Monitor | 10 | ₹15,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| Business Keyboard | 10 | ₹2,000 | ₹20,000 |
| Wireless Mouse | 10 | ₹1,500 | ₹15,000 |
| **Total** | **40 units** | | **₹7,85,000** |

## Expected Business Flow

The transaction should progress through the following stages:

1. Customer places an order.
2. Order information is captured.
3. Customer and order details are validated.
4. Product availability is checked.
5. The order proceeds through fulfillment.
6. Products are shipped to the customer.
7. An invoice is generated.
8. Customer makes payment.
9. Payment is recorded and matched against the customer receivable.
10. The transaction is considered complete after successful payment processing and reconciliation.

## Business Outcome

The expected outcome is a successfully completed customer transaction with:

- Correct order information
- Successful fulfillment
- Successful shipment
- Accurate invoice
- Correct payment recording
- Proper receivables closure
