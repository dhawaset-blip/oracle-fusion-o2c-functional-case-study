# Company Profile

## 1. Company Name

**NovaTech Electronics Pvt. Ltd.**

## 2. Industry

Consumer Electronics / B2B Technology

## 3. Business Model

NovaTech Electronics Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional B2B electronics company that sells technology products to business customers.

The company receives customer orders, processes fulfillment, ships products, generates invoices and collects customer payments.

## 6. Business Challenge

NovaTech currently relies on disconnected and partially manual activities for managing customer orders, fulfillment, invoicing and payment tracking.

This creates potential issues such as:

- Duplicate data entry
- Delays in order processing
- Inconsistent information between departments
- Invoice errors
- Limited order-status visibility
- Difficulty tracking outstanding payments

## 7. Business Goal

NovaTech wants to establish a standardized and integrated Order-to-Cash process that improves:

- Process consistency
- Data visibility
- Order tracking
- Invoice accuracy
- Payment tracking
- Coordination between business functions

## 8. Primary Case Study Customer

For this project, the primary business scenario will use:

**ABC Solutions Pvt. Ltd.**

ABC Solutions will place a multi-product order with NovaTech, and the complete transaction will be followed through the O2C lifecycle.
